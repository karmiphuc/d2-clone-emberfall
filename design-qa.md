# v0.17 expanded, populated wilderness

Scope: larger playable Blood Moor, populated camp, outer encounters and usable navigation. [Previous review](docs/qa-v0.16.md).

## Evidence and findings

Reviewed [camp residents](docs/screenshots/camp-v0.17.webp), [old road](docs/screenshots/old-road-v0.17.webp), [burial ground](docs/screenshots/graves-v0.17.webp), [Cinder Camp](docs/screenshots/cinder-camp-v0.17.webp), [East Watch](docs/screenshots/east-watch-v0.17.webp), [caravan](docs/screenshots/caravan-v0.17.webp), [area map](docs/screenshots/area-map-v0.17.webp) and [phone map](docs/screenshots/phone-map-v0.17.webp). Full local PNG captures remain in `/workspace/emberfall-design/v17-review`.

1. **P1, improved — map was a small encounter arena.** The playable footprint expands from 36.4×28 to 84×68 units, about 5.6 times the area. Shared bounds drive collision, heap-based navigation, minimap scaling and outer-drop save validation. Browser checks route from the entrance to all ten regions; a unit check crosses the whole footprint around a separating obstacle without cutting corners.
2. **P1, improved — world felt sparse.** Sixty extra enemies form ten local packs around graveyards, abandoned camps, ruined watches, fields and a lost caravan. Four packs include an additional elite. Outer foes patrol locally while unengaged. Six camp residents walk normal waypoint routes. The Moor gains 1,500 instanced grass patches, hundreds of instanced shrubs, denser treelines and merged ground clutter. Trees fade when they obscure the hero; distant actors are hidden from presentation conservatively according to camera size.
3. **P1, preserved — larger world must keep early progression playable.** Original twelve enemy IDs and positions remain the old-road objective. Akara's bounty and fresh hunts require those foes and their drops, rather than every optional pack. Old saves remain valid. UI explains that refreshing resets the entire Moor, including outer spoils. Additional defeats and loot persist through retreat/reload. Level cap remains six; the rest of Act I is still unfinished.
4. **P2, improved — distant destinations are hard to navigate.** MAP / M opens a larger map. Click the map or one of ten numbered destination buttons to issue a normal walking order; no teleport is used. Phone buttons use a consistent accessible name, fit the viewport and retain readable names. M or Escape closes the map. The map is not a pause screen; combat continues.
5. **P2, improved — increased density must control render cost.** Grass/shrubs are instanced, roads/rocks/walls are merged, and distant foreground-fade work is skipped. The reviewed East Watch scene used 116 draw calls and 31,624 triangles, with 35 of 72 enemy roots within the conservative presentation radius. This software WebGL snapshot is not a hardware FPS guarantee. Existing four-facing sprites and inferred weapon-edge softness remain.

## Verification

60 unit tests passed. Six initial browser checks passed for camp, original expedition/retreat and motion continuity. Expanded-world checks passed for all ten routes, desktop/phone map commands, moving camp residents and outer-pack patrol/combat/loot/reload. The existing level-six repeat-hunt check passed. An initial new phone test failed because it searched for a keyboard hint hidden on phones; the button now has a stable accessible name and the corrected check passed. Captures reported no console errors or phone overflow. Production packaging passed. Full regression passed: 60 unit tests and all 25 browser tests (85 total). Production checks passed through normal controls: mercenary weapon/armor ownership survives reload, the phone UI fits, automatic attacks defeat a Blood Moor enemy without target clicks, and the enlarged map exposes all ten destination buttons and issues walking commands. No console errors or missing assets. The standalone check passed the same normal-control checks, with no external game-image requests. The packaged file is 31,127,537 bytes. Publication is verified against the public bundle after the GitHub workflow completes.
