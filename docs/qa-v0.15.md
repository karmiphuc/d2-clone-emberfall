# v0.15 stable sprite lighting and complete action variants

Scope: movement and attack presentation for hero sword/axe, six companion roles and three creature types. [Previous review](qa-v0.14.md).

## Evidence and findings

Captured all three hero walk/attack sets at matching runtime poses and reviewed the contact sheets: [movement variants](screenshots/walk-variants-v0.15.webp), [attack variants](screenshots/attack-variants-v0.15.webp), [camp movement](screenshots/camp-walk-v0.15.webp), [combat](screenshots/combat-v0.15.webp), [phone preview](screenshots/phone-preview-v0.15.webp). Recording/captures remain in `/workspace/emberfall-design/v15-review`. Local Chromium/Playwright supplies browser evidence.

1. **P1, improved — movement brightness/opacity pulses.** The original hero sword first-facing gait varied from approximately 54 to 62 encoded luminance units. After per-facing diffuse exposure correction, the decoded WebP first-facing range is 0.33; axe is 0.28, companion A first actor is 0.35. A consistent coverage matte makes painted surfaces opaque and retains narrow soft silhouette edges. Stationary atlases receive the same matte treatment. Baked exposure range across all actor/facing/variant sequences is at most 0.493 before final WebP encoding. Real damage/selection highlights retain their intended feedback.
2. **P1, improved — repeated actions feel identical.** Three complete sets now exist for each movement and attack action. Original alternate hero keys add low-side and high-diagonal sword/axe actions, with 33 playback frames per set. Companion/creature alternatives and all gait alternatives use deterministic anchored pose deformation, weight/lean/recovery variation and smooth gait timing curves. Those are derived sets, not independently painted animation in every frame.
3. **P1, verified locally — randomized selection must not jumble motion.** Independent shuffled bags use each of the three sets before refill and avoid immediate repetition. Choices stay fixed through preparation/contact/recovery; enemy windup/release share a set, duplicate combat notifications cannot reselect mid-strike. Gaits change at starts or complete stride seams. Contact/damage remains at the same clock and hero playback frame 12. Simulation, damage and save data never use this animation randomness.
4. **P2, improved — additional variants can increase texture cost.** Three horizontal four-facing banks share one atlas/source allocation. Hero cells are 128px; other actors 96px. The standalone build is 31,117,762 bytes, smaller than v0.14. Runtime UVs, transitions and silhouette picking account for the selected bank.
5. **P3, remaining — fidelity of source anatomy and inferred edges.** Four facings, limited underlying companion gaits and reused death art remain. Some intermediate weapon edges soften or double around occlusions, and enlarged previews reveal resolution/proportion limits. Brightness normalization addresses the reported exposure pulse; it does not make every painted material detail identical between poses.
